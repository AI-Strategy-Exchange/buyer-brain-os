# Brief

Write this before anything runs. Every step reads it. A vague brief produces a vague card.

## What I sell
Every product, service, and package you sell, who pays, and price if you have one.

## How I work
What happens after a client says yes, step by step, and what you do that most sellers skip. The
yardstick competitors are matched against.

## How clients find me
How recent clients found you, who introduced them, and what was going on right before they
reached out.

## A client I want more of (context only, does not steer the analysis)
One specific kind of buyer. If you sell to two very different buyers, run the folder once for
each.

## Where my buyers are
Country or region, local or online.

## Who I think I compete with
Your guess. Competitor-map tests it.

## Competitors to read
Filled by competitor-map after the buyer card exists.

## My current positioning (baseline, not audited)
Your site, LinkedIn, booking page, the pitch you give out loud, your avatar or ICP, anything you
pasted or pointed to. The cross-check holds the gaps against it. Not audited.

## Buyer material
What is in `inputs/transcripts/`, `inputs/summaries/`, and `inputs/written/`, and your name on
the calls.

## What I believe today
Two or three sentences about why this buyer buys from you. Write it now, before the research.
The cross-check compares against it, so do not edit it later.
